HOW TO CARE FOR
YOUR PRESS-ON

← APPLY MEASURE →
1

Daily Maintenance

Keep your press-ons clean by gently wiping them with a soft cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals, bleach, and acetone-based products near the nail area.

2

Water & Activities

Avoid prolonged water exposure — dishes, swimming, or long showers. Wear gloves when doing household chores to significantly extend wear time.

3

Proper Storage

When not in use, store your press-on nails in the original packaging in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to preserve colour and shape.

4

Safe Removal & Reuse

Soak nails in warm water for 5–10 minutes to loosen the adhesive. Gently peel from the side — never force them off. ONNAILS press-ons are designed for multiple uses!

5

When to Replace

Replace your press-ons when they become chipped, cracked, or lose their shine. With proper care, each set can last weeks of wear and multiple uses.
