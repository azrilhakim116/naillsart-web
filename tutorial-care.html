HOW TO CARE FOR
YOUR PRESS-ON

Maintenance Tips

Keep your press-ons clean by gently washing them with soap and water. Avoid harsh chemicals and excessive water exposure.

Storage

Store your press-on nails in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Use the original packaging when possible.

Longevity

With proper care, press-on nails can be reused multiple times. Remove them gently by soaking in warm water to loosen adhesive.

When to Replace

Replace your press-ons if they become damaged, chipped, or no longer fit properly. Quality nails from ONNAILS are built to last!
