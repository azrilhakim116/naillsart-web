HOW TO APPLY YOUR
NAILS

1. Natural Nail Preparation (Prep):

Thoroughly clean your natural nails to remove oil, cuticles, and natural shine. Push back or trim cuticles, lightly buff the nail surface, and then wipe with alcohol or a nail cleanser. Proper prep ensures the artificial nail adheres strongly and lasts longer.

2. Adhesive Application:

Determine the type of adhesive you will use (liquid nail glue for maximum durability, or adhesive tabs for temporary wear). Apply the adhesive sufficiently either on the underside of the faux nail (the part that will stick to your natural nail), directly onto your natural nail, or both if using liquid glue.

3. Placement and Pressing:

Position the artificial nail precisely just under the cuticle line (without touching the skin) and ensure it is straight. Press the nail down firmly for 10 to 30 seconds (depending on the glue type) to eliminate air pockets and ensure the adhesive fully bonds. Avoid contact with water for at least 1-2 hours after application.
