HOW TO APPLY
YOUR NAILS

Step 1: Prepare Your Nails

Clean your natural nails and file them short. Push back your cuticles and file the surface slightly to help adhesive bond better.

Step 2: Size Correctly

Test each nail size to ensure a snug fit. The press-on nail should sit flush against your skin with no gaps.

Step 3: Apply Adhesive

Apply a small amount of nail adhesive or adhesive tabs. Press down firmly for 10-15 seconds for secure hold.

Step 4: Allow to Set

Wait several minutes before exposing your nails to water to ensure the adhesive is fully cured.
