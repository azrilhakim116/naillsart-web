Our Story

THE NAIL ART BUSINESS ONNAILS WAS OFFICIALLY ESTABLISHED ON OCTOBER 23, 2023, BY FITRIA NURAENI SUHERMAN. IT ORIGINATED FROM THE FOUNDER'S PERSONAL DISSATISFACTION WITH THE QUALITY AND DURABILITY OF FALSE NAILS AVAILABLE IN THE MARKET. DRIVEN BY A HOBBY AND THE DESIRE TO CREATE A SUPERIOR, REPEATEDLY REUSABLE PRODUCT, FITRIA SELF-TAUGHT THE TECHNIQUES OF MAKING PRESS-ON NAILS, MAKING SUPERIOR QUALITY THE MAIN PHILOSOPHY OF HER BUSINESS. SINCE ITS FOUNDING, ONNAILS HAS NOT ONLY FOCUSED ON PRODUCTS BUT HAS ALSO SHOWN SIGNIFICANT PERFORMANCE IN THE SERVICE AND RETAIL MARKETS; THE COMPANY SUCCESSFULLY SELLS OVER 700 SETS OF PRESS-ON NAILS AND PROVIDES MORE THAN 200 NAIL ART SERVICES ANNUALLY, PROVING THAT A FOCUS ON QUALITY AND THE REUSABLE VALUE PROPOSITION IS WELL-RECEIVED BY THE MARKET
