PROFILE-ON NAILS

THE NAIL ART BUSINESS ONNAILS WAS OFFICIALLY ESTABLISHED ON OCTOBER 23, 2023, BY FITRIA NURAENI SUHERMAN. IT ORIGINATED FROM THE FOUNDER'S PERSONAL DISSATISFACTION WITH THE QUALITY AND DURABILITY OF FALSE NAILS AVAILABLE IN THE MARKET.

DRIVEN BY A HOBBY AND THE DESIRE TO CREATE A SUPERIOR, REPEATEDLY REUSABLE PRODUCT, FITRIA SELF-TAUGHT THE TECHNIQUES OF MAKING PRESS-ON NAILS, MAKING SUPERIOR QUALITY THE MAIN PHILOSOPHY OF HER BUSINESS.

SINCE ITS FOUNDING, ONNAILS HAS SUCCESSFULLY SOLD OVER 700 SETS OF PRESS-ON NAILS AND PROVIDED MORE THAN 200 NAIL ART SERVICES ANNUALLY.
